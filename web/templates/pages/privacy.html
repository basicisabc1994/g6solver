Privacy

Your work, your data and your prompts are yours, so nothing about G6 or this site is built to collect them.

This website collects nothing about you. It is a set of static pages. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics or tracking, has no forms and no accounts, and keeps no record of your visit. The company that hosts the pages may keep the standard short-lived server logs that any web host keeps.

The page fonts come from Google Fonts and some scripts from public code libraries (cdnjs and jsDelivr). Your browser fetches them directly from those services, under their own privacy terms.

G6 itself

G6 is software you run on your own computer. It sends nothing to us: there is no account, no telemetry and no service of ours behind it. If you connect G6 to an AI model provider, what you send goes to that provider under your own agreement with them.
