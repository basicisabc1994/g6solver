# Reason over rules and abstractions.

<!-- route:rules -->
- **rules.** What follows from facts and rules: datalog (sessions keep facts over a fixed program), prolog and
  graph_reasoning, through `g6_invoke` (`references/rules.md`).
<!-- /route:rules -->

<!-- route:abstraction -->
- **abstraction.** Only where the person registered the separate server, `python -P -m g6.abstraction.server`: its
  tools search a design space for abstractions that hold up (`references/abstraction.md`).
<!-- /route:abstraction -->

Component families: cogarch, provers, domains.

Use `g6_describe("logic")` for registered operations and call templates. Read the named component with
`g6_describe` before choosing fields. Read `g6_describe("g6_invoke")` before invoking an operation.
The list is guidance, never admission to a sensitive operation or consent to share a source.
