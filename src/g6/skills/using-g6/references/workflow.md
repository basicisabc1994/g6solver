## Workflow route

- **workflow.** G6's local record of its own review work is on by default; `G6_WORKFLOW_RECORD=off` turns it off.
  `g6_workflow_export` writes `inspection.json` and `RECORD.md`; `g6_workflow_correct`, `g6_workflow_delete`, `g6_suggestion_decide` and
  `g6_feedback`. Never write its files or answer its dialogs for the person.
