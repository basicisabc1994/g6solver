Use `g6_describe` with a family for its members and call templates, then with a tool name for its full guidance.
Not sure? `g6_select` or `g6_find` returns ranked candidates and their families; an explicit limit is honoured.

## Routes

A tool named here that your server does not list is absent: say so, use `g6_find`, and do not call it again.

Read `g6_allowances` before optional fan-out. Stale or missing readings never justify increased use.
Never answer consent dialogs for the person. Preserve returned reliability and limitations.
